# ============ CHILDREN OF CRONUS & RHEA ============
@region olympus
@type olympian
@src Hesiod, Theogony 453–506
hestia | Hestia | Vesta | cronus+rhea | Goddess of the hearth; first swallowed and last disgorged, so both eldest and youngest.
  m: Hestia swore to stay a virgin, refusing Poseidon and Apollo, and Zeus gave her the centre of every home and the first share of every sacrifice. Later lists sometimes replace her among the Twelve with Dionysus.
  r: First to be swallowed and last to come out, I am both eldest and youngest; the first share of every offering is mine. ;; Hestia
demeter | Demeter | Ceres | cronus+rhea | Goddess of grain and harvest; mother of Persephone.
  s: Hesiod, Theogony 454, 912–914; Homeric Hymn to Demeter
  m: When Hades carried off Persephone, Demeter searched the earth with torches and let the crops fail. Zeus had Persephone returned, but because she had eaten pomegranate seeds she spends part of each year below, and those months are winter.
  r: While my daughter is below, nothing grows; that is why you have winter. ;; Demeter
hera | Hera | Juno | cronus+rhea | Queen of the gods and goddess of marriage; wife and sister of Zeus.
  m: Hera pursued Zeus's lovers and their children: Leto, Io, Semele, Alcmene and Heracles above all. She sided with the Greeks at Troy after Paris judged against her.
  r: I sent two serpents to the cradle of my husband's son and a gadfly after the heifer he loved. ;; Hera
hades | Hades | Pluto / Dis Pater | cronus+rhea | King of the underworld and the dead.
  T: deity
  R: under
  m: When the brothers divided the world by lot, Hades got the underworld. He carried off Persephone with Zeus's consent. His helmet of invisibility, made by the Cyclopes, was lent to Perseus.
  r: I drew the dark third of the world by lot, and I won my bride with six pomegranate seeds. ;; Hades
poseidon | Poseidon | Neptune | cronus+rhea | God of the sea, earthquakes and horses.
  R: sea
  m: Poseidon competed with Athena for Athens, striking the Acropolis rock to bring out a salt spring; Athena's olive tree won. He hounded Odysseus for blinding his son Polyphemus.
  r: My trident struck a salt spring from the Acropolis, but an olive tree beat me to the city. ;; Poseidon
zeus | Zeus | Jupiter | cronus+rhea | King of gods and men, god of sky, thunder and justice.
  m: Hidden on Crete as a baby, Zeus grew up, forced Cronus to disgorge his siblings, and with the Cyclopes' thunderbolts and the Hundred-Handers won the ten-year war against the Titans. He then defeated the Giants and Typhon and divided the world with his brothers.
  r: A stone in swaddling clothes was swallowed in my place, and I grew up to overthrow the swallower. ;; Zeus
chiron | Chiron | Chiron | cronus+philyra | Wise, immortal centaur; teacher of Achilles, Jason and Asclepius.
  T: creature
  R: earth
  s: Apollodorus, Bibliotheca 1.2.4, 2.5.4
  m: Heracles accidentally wounded Chiron with an arrow poisoned by the Hydra. Unable to die and in endless pain, Chiron gave up his immortality in exchange for Prometheus's release, and Zeus placed him among the stars.
  r: A friend's poisoned arrow gave me a wound I could not die of, so I traded my immortality for a Titan's freedom. ;; Chiron

  s: Hesiod, Theogony 927–929
  m: Hera threw Hephaestus from Olympus for being lame (or Zeus did, for taking Hera's side). He took revenge with a golden throne that trapped her; only Dionysus could bring him back, drunk on a mule. He built the gods' halls, Achilles' armour, Pandora and the bronze giant Talos.

  m: Born on Delos, Apollo killed the Python and took over Delphi. He served the mortal Admetus as a herdsman as punishment for killing the Cyclopes, flayed Marsyas after a music contest, and brought plague on the Greeks at Troy for the insult to his priest Chryses.
  r: I killed the serpent at the navel of the world, and I speak through a priestess who sits on a tripod. ;; Apollo
artemis | Artemis | Diana | zeus+leto | Virgin huntress, goddess of wild animals, the young and childbirth.
  s: Hesiod, Theogony 918–920; Callimachus, Hymn 3
  m: Artemis turned Actaeon into a stag when he saw her bathing. With Apollo she killed Niobe's children, and she demanded the sacrifice of Iphigenia at Aulis.
  r: A hunter saw me bathing, and his own hounds tore him apart in the shape of a stag. ;; Artemis
hermes | Hermes | Mercury | zeus+maia | Messenger god of travellers, thieves, trade and guide of the dead.
  s: Hesiod, Theogony 938–939; Homeric Hymn to Hermes
  m: On the day he was born, Hermes invented the lyre from a tortoise shell and stole fifty of Apollo's cattle, walking them backwards. He traded the lyre to Apollo for the herd. He killed hundred-eyed Argus to free Io, and guides souls to Hades.
  r: On the day I was born I made a lyre from a tortoise and stole my brother's cattle by walking them backwards. ;; Hermes
dionysus | Dionysus | Bacchus / Liber | zeus+semele | God of wine, ecstasy and theatre; the twice-born.
  s: Hesiod, Theogony 940–942; Euripides, Bacchae
  a: zeus+persephone | Orphic tradition (reborn from Zagreus)
  m: Semele asked to see Zeus in his true form and was burned up. Zeus sewed the unborn Dionysus into his thigh until birth. Dionysus travelled the world spreading the vine, turned Tyrrhenian pirates into dolphins, and punished Pentheus for rejecting him.
  r: Born once from my mother's ashes and a second time from my father's thigh, I turned pirates into dolphins. ;; Dionysus
aphrodite | Aphrodite | Venus | uranus | Goddess of love, beauty and desire, born of the sea foam.

  m: Aphrodite came ashore at Cyprus (or Cythera). She married Hephaestus but loved Ares, promised Paris the most beautiful woman in the world, and gave Hippomenes the golden apples to win Atalanta.
  r: I rose from the foam off Cyprus, and a shepherd prince gave me a golden apple in exchange for another man's wife. ;; Aphrodite

  R: under
